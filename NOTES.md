The first release of Auditly, a free, private app privacy and permission auditor for Android.

- A privacy grade from A to F for every app, with every reason shown, and a privacy score for the phone
- Every permission explained in plain English: what it allows, how it can be misused and who normally needs it
- Tracker scanner covering 69 known advertising, analytics and crash-reporting libraries
- Special access audit, apps that don't fit their kind, and monitoring-software patterns flagged
- Fix it, a step-by-step list of the changes that matter most; Compare two apps; Unused apps
- Optional watcher with alerts for risky changes, and an encrypted timeline
- PDF, spreadsheet and JSON reports, a privacy score widget, app lock and four themes
- Optional Power mode with Shizuku
- No adverts, no account and no internet access

Needs Android 10 or later. Download `Auditly.apk` below and open it to install.
